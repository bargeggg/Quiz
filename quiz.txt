Bank Soal Pilihan Ganda (13 Soal - Tanpa Penjelasan):

SOAL 1
“The city implemented a strict congestion pricing policy; specifically, it levied a daily fee on all non-electric vehicles entering the downtown core during peak hours.”
Bagaimana hubungan klausa kedua dengan klausa pertama pada kalimat di atas?
A) Addition, karena memperkenalkan kebijakan baru yang sama sekali terpisah.
B) Definition, karena menjelaskan arti kamus dari kata congestion pricing.
C) Clarification, karena merumuskan kembali pernyataan umum dengan detail operasional yang presisi.
D) Example, karena mencantumkan contoh acak dari kendaraan listrik.
Kunci Jawaban: C

SOAL 2
“Renewable energy sources significantly reduce carbon footprints. Furthermore, they foster long-term economic independence by insulating countries from volatile global fossil fuel markets.”
Hubungan logis apa yang ditandai oleh kata “Furthermore” pada teks di atas?
A) Clarification, karena menjelaskan mekanisme fisik dari pengurangan karbon.
B) Addition, karena memperkenalkan keuntungan independen dan kumulatif lainnya dari energi terbarukan.
C) Definition, karena mendefinisikan arti dari kemandirian ekonomi.
D) Example, karena memberikan contoh jenis sumber energi terbarukan yang spesifik.
Kunci Jawaban: B

SOAL 3
“Neurolinguistics is an interdisciplinary field that investigates the neural mechanisms in the human brain that control the comprehension, production, and acquisition of language.”
Hubungan ide apa yang paling mendominasi kalimat di atas?
A) Definition, karena secara eksplisit menguraikan arti dan ruang lingkup dasar dari istilah neurolinguistics.
B) Addition, karena menambahkan detail kumulatif tentang fungsi otak manusia.
C) Example, karena mencontohkan salah satu cabang dari ilmu psikologi modern.
D) Clarification, karena memperbaiki kesalahpahaman linguistik yang terjadi sebelumnya.
Kunci Jawaban: A

SOAL 4
“The hospital is within walking distance of the bus station, while the airport is far from the city center.” What is the difference between the meanings of “within” and “far” in the sentence above?
A. Within menunjukkan tempat yang bersebelahan, sedangkan far menunjukkan tempat di dalam batas tertentu.
B. Within menunjukkan jarak yang masih dapat dijangkau dengan berjalan kaki, sedangkan far menunjukkan jarak yang jauh.
C. Within menunjukkan arah pergerakan, sedangkan far menunjukkan posisi yang berlawanan.
D. Within menunjukkan kesimpulan lokasi, sedangkan far menunjukkan urutan tempat.
Kunci Jawaban: B

SOAL 5
“Call me as soon as you arrive at the airport.” The phrase “as soon as” belongs to which category of time transitions?
A. Before
B. Simultaneous
C. After
D. Immediate succession
Kunci Jawaban: D

SOAL 6
“The road was flooded. _____, the school decided to cancel classes.” Which transition word best completes the sentence?
A. In summary
B. Meanwhile
C. Therefore
D. Afterwards
Kunci Jawaban: C

SOAL 7
Kalimat mana yang punya hubungan "comparison"?
A. The red car is fast, but the blue car is quite slow
B. If you drive the car too fast, you will get a fine
C. Both the red car and the blue car are incredibly fast
D. Even though the car is old, it is still very fast
Kunci Jawaban: C

SOAL 8
_______ studying all night, he still failed the math test.
A. Despite
B. Similarly
C. As long as
D. However
Kunci Jawaban: A

SOAL 9
"You can play video games as long as you finish your homework first." termasuk kalimat?
A. Concession
B. Comparison
C. Contrast
D. Condition
Kunci Jawaban: D

SOAL 10
Look at the keyword “due to”: “The flight was delayed due to thick fog.” What does “thick fog” represent?
A. Purpose
B. Result/Effect
C. Reason
D. Cause
Kunci Jawaban: D

SOAL 11
A citizen says, “My reason for asking for transparency in the MBG budget is that people should know how public money is used.” What is the reason for the citizen's request?
A. The citizen asks for budget transparency
B. The citizen wants public spending to be open to public scrutiny
C. People knowing how public money is used
D. The MBG program uses public money
Kunci Jawaban: B

SOAL 12
Look at the keyword “to”: “Maya exercises every morning to stay healthy.” What does “to stay healthy” express?
A. Reason
B. Purpose
C. Cause
D. Result/Effect
Kunci Jawaban: B

SOAL 13
The IHSG is falling, investors are starting to panic, and many people are worried that the value of their investments will decrease. In this cause and effect situation, what does the decline in the IHSG represent?
A. Purpose, because the decline in the IHSG is the goal of investors when they invest in the stock market.
B. Reason, because the decline in the IHSG explains why investors start buying stocks.
C. Cause, because the decline in the IHSG causes investors to become worried.
D. Result/Effect, because the decline in the IHSG is the outcome of investors' concerns about the economic situation.
Kunci Jawaban: C
